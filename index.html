hi, it's charl!

I'm Charlyn Grace Navidad, a 4th-year BSIT-Multimedia Arts & Animation student at National University passionate about graphic design, photography, and multimedia production. I enjoy turning ideas into purposeful visual content through creativity and technology.

When it comes to design, I believe that simple is powerful. I enjoy creating work that is clean, easy to understand, and meaningful, proving that even the simplest designs can leave a lasting impact.
